How to download from Instagram

Copy the URL

Open Instagram, tap the share menu on a post, Reel, Story or carousel, and copy its link.

Paste the link

Come back to ReelKit, paste the link into the field and press the button.

Save the result

Download the file, or copy the caption or save the audio as MP3.

Share to ReelKit: setup

1. Install the app

In Chrome tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app. “Add to Home screen” is not enough. If you added it earlier, remove that icon and install again.

2. Share a Reel

In Instagram open a Reel, tap Share, choose Share to…, then pick ReelKit. Look under More if you don’t see it.

3. Still missing?

Tick auto-detect at the top, copy the Reel link in Instagram, then open ReelKit.

Frequently asked questions

Is downloading from Instagram legal?

Saving public posts for personal use is generally fine, but the copyright belongs to the creator. Keep files private unless you have permission to reuse them.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Only public posts work. If you can’t see the post while logged out, ReelKit can’t fetch it either.

Are my links stored?

No. Temporary files are removed after download or within an hour.
