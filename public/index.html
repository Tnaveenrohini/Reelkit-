About WaveRise

Free, no account needed

WaveRise is a toolkit for saving and reusing public posts. Paste the link of a public Instagram post, Reel, photo or carousel, or an X (Twitter) video post, and WaveRise fetches it for you.

Beyond downloads, WaveRise turns a Reel’s sound into an MP3, makes ringtones for Android and iPhone, trims clips, and copies captions and hashtags with a small hashtag toolkit.

How it works

When you submit a link, our server reads the public post and streams the file to your device. There are no accounts. Temporary files are deleted as soon as your request finishes.

Independent service

WaveRise is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Instagram or Meta. Please save only content you own or have permission to use.

Frequently asked questions

Is WaveRise free?

Yes. You don’t need an account or any login.

Is downloading from Instagram legal?

Saving public posts for personal use is generally fine, but the copyright belongs to the creator. Keep files private unless you have permission to reuse them.

Can I download from private accounts?

No. Only public posts work. If you can’t see the post while logged out, WaveRise can’t fetch it either.

Why do I see “Couldn’t read this link”?

The post may be private, deleted or not a supported link type, or Instagram may be limiting requests for a moment. Check the link opens in a private browser tab, then try again.

Can I download Stories, Highlights or profile pictures?

No. WaveRise doesn’t offer them, out of respect for people’s privacy. It only saves public posts, Reels and carousels, and only when you paste a link.

What does Louder sound do?

It evens out quiet or uneven audio so MP3s play at a comfortable, steady volume. You’ll find it on the Video to MP3 tool.

Which links work?

Public Instagram posts (/p/), Reels (/reel/) and IGTV (/tv/), and X (Twitter) posts such as x.com/name/status/123. Threads and YouTube links aren’t supported yet.

Does the X downloader save photos?

WaveRise saves videos and GIFs from X posts. Photo-only posts aren’t supported. Protected accounts and posts that need a login can’t be saved.

What quality do I get?

Videos download in the best MP4 quality Instagram provides. MP3 files are 192 kbps. Clips are re-encoded as H.264 MP4.

How many hashtags can I use?

Instagram currently allows up to 5 hashtags per post. The hashtag toolkit warns you if you go over.

How do I share my ringtones and wallpapers?

Open Explore, sign in with Google or your email, and tap Upload. Ringtones can be MP3, M4A, AAC or OGG files up to 60 seconds; you can also make one from any Reel with the Ringtone tool and tap Publish to Explore. Everything on Explore is free to download, like and share, and you can follow creators you like.

How do I install WaveRise as an app?

In Chrome, open the three-dot menu and tap Install app, or use the Install button at the bottom of the page.

Are my links stored?

No. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What we handle

You can use WaveRise’s downloaders and tools without an account. When you submit an Instagram or X link, it is sent to our server so we can read the public post and return the file, audio, clip or caption you asked for.

No Instagram login

WaveRise never logs in to Instagram and never asks for or stores your Instagram password or login. It does not download Stories, Highlights or profile pictures.

Temporary files

Videos are streamed to you. Clips, ringtones and Reels are briefly created on the server and deleted when your request finishes. Photos you choose for Carousel to Reel are deleted as soon as the video is made. We do not keep copies of the content you process.

Community accounts (Explore)

An account is only needed to upload, like, follow or report on Explore. You sign in with Google or with a link sent to your email. We keep your email address private; it is never shown to anyone. Your username, name, profile picture (from Google, if you use it), bio, uploads, likes and follows are public. Reports are seen only by our admins. Uploaded wallpapers have their location and camera details removed. You can edit your profile, delete any upload, or delete your whole account from your profile at any time; deleting the account removes your profile, uploads, likes and follows. Accounts, uploads and files are stored with Supabase, our database and file host.

AI tools

Background Remover, Smart Crop, Festival Frames and the sticker cut-out run entirely on your device: your photo is never uploaded. The first time you use them, your browser downloads the AI files from jsDelivr, a public file network. The Caption Writer sends the text you type (or the caption of the post link you give) to our server; if AI writing is switched on, that text is passed to Groq to write the captions. Don’t type private information into it.

On your device

WaveRise does not set cookies. It saves your saved hashtag sets and the auto-detect setting in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings. If you turn on auto-detect, the page reads your clipboard only to look for an Instagram link.

Third parties

  • Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes requests and may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
  • AI files load from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address.
  • Caption Writer text may be processed by Groq to write captions.
  • Community accounts, uploads and files are stored by Supabase. Google sign-in is handled by Google.
  • Content is fetched from the original platform’s servers on your behalf.

We show no ads. To see which pages and tools are used, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, doesn’t follow you to other sites and only counts visits (the page, your country, and the type of device and browser). It doesn’t see your links, photos or files.

Children

WaveRise is not directed at children under 13.
