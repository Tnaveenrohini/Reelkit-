    About WaveRise

    Free tools, made in India. No account needed.

    WaveRise is a free set of everyday tools for people who fill in online forms, share on WhatsApp and make things for family and friends. It started as a way to save public Reels and has grown into 21 tools in English, हिन्दी, தமிழ் and తెలుగు.

    What you can do

    • Forms and documents: make a photo or signature fit an exam or job form, print passport photos at home, add your name and date under a photo, and turn document photos into one PDF.
    • Photos and greetings: remove a background, make festival greetings with your own photo, daily Status images and WhatsApp stickers.
    • Video and audio: turn a video’s sound into an MP3, make a ringtone, trim a clip, make a GIF, shrink a video for WhatsApp and split long videos into Status parts.
    • Save from a link: save public Instagram, Facebook and X videos and photos.

    How we treat your files

    Photo and document tools run on your phone, so your pictures are never uploaded. When a tool needs a link, our server reads the public post and sends the file straight to you; temporary files are deleted when your request finishes. There are no accounts, and we never ask for an Instagram or Facebook password.

    What we don’t do

    WaveRise doesn’t save Stories, Highlights, profile pictures or anything from private accounts, to respect people’s privacy. Please save only content you own or have permission to use.

    Independent service

    WaveRise is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Instagram, Facebook, Meta, X, YouTube or Google.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is WaveRise free?

    Yes. You don’t need an account or any login.

    Is downloading from Instagram legal?

    Saving public posts for personal use is generally fine, but the copyright belongs to the creator. Keep files private unless you have permission to reuse them.

    Can I download from private accounts?

    No. Only public posts work. If you can’t see the post while logged out, WaveRise can’t fetch it either.

    Why do I see “Couldn’t read this link”?

    The post may be private, deleted or not a supported link type, or Instagram may be limiting requests for a moment. Check the link opens in a private browser tab, then try again.

    Can I download Stories, Highlights or profile pictures?

    No. WaveRise doesn’t offer them, out of respect for people’s privacy. It only saves public posts, Reels and carousels, and only when you paste a link.

    What does Louder sound do?

    It evens out quiet or uneven audio so MP3s play at a comfortable, steady volume. You’ll find it on the Video to MP3 tool.

    Which links work?

    Public Instagram posts (/p/), Reels (/reel/) and IGTV (/tv/), public Facebook videos and Reels (including fb.watch and share links), and X (Twitter) posts such as x.com/name/status/123. Public Threads posts work on the Threads downloader. YouTube links aren’t supported yet.

    Does the X downloader save photos?

    WaveRise saves videos and GIFs from X posts. Photo-only posts aren’t supported. Protected accounts and posts that need a login can’t be saved.

    What quality do I get?

    Videos download in the best MP4 quality Instagram provides. MP3 files are 192 kbps. Clips are re-encoded as H.264 MP4.

    How many hashtags can I use?

    Instagram currently allows up to 5 hashtags per post. The hashtag toolkit warns you if you go over.

    How do I install WaveRise as an app?

    In Chrome, open the three-dot menu and tap Install app, or use the Install button at the bottom of the page.

    Are my links stored?

    No. See the Privacy Policy for details.

    Do the photo tools upload my pictures?

    No. Photo MB to KB, Passport Photo, Signature Maker, Photo + Name & Date, Photos to PDF, Background Remover, Festival Frames and Sticker Maker work on your phone. Your photos never leave it.

    Which languages does WaveRise speak?

    English, Hindi, Tamil and Telugu. Tap the EN button at the top to switch.

    Where can I get more help?

    Read the Guides, ask WaveRise Help from the button at the bottom of any page, or contact us.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 2 November 2026

    What we handle

    You can use WaveRise’s downloaders and tools without an account. When you submit an Instagram, Facebook or X link, it is sent to our server so we can read the public post and return the file, audio, clip or caption you asked for.

    No Instagram login

    WaveRise never logs in to Instagram and never asks for or stores your Instagram password or login. It does not download Stories, Highlights or profile pictures.

    Temporary files

    Videos are streamed to you. Clips, ringtones and Reels are briefly created on the server and deleted when your request finishes. We do not keep copies of the content you process.

    AI tools

    Background Remover, Festival Frames and the sticker cut-out run entirely on your device: your photo is never uploaded. The first time you use them, your browser downloads the AI files from jsDelivr, a public file network.

    On your device

    WaveRise itself does not set cookies. It saves your saved hashtag sets and the auto-detect setting in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device and you can clear it in your browser settings. If you turn on auto-detect, the page reads your clipboard only to look for an Instagram link.

    Third parties

    • Our hosting provider (Vercel) processes requests and may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.
    • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.
    • AI files load from jsDelivr, which receives your IP address.
    • Content is fetched from the original platform’s servers on your behalf.

    To see which pages and tools are used, we use Vercel Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, doesn’t follow you to other sites and only counts visits (the page, your country, and the type of device and browser). It doesn’t see your links, photos or files. We may also use Google Analytics to understand which pages are popular; it uses cookies and you can block it with your browser’s privacy settings or an ad blocker. We never send Google your links, photos or files.

    Advertising

    WaveRise may show ads from Google AdSense on some pages, such as the photo and form tools and the guides. Ads are never shown on the pages that save posts from a link.
